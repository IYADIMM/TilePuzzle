# UI and engagement

Reusable Phaser controls now use a raised visual plate plus a four-pixel press translation and smooth return, instead of scale-only feedback. This applies through the common button helpers used by Home, Journey, Collections, Shop, boosters, pause, settings and rewards.

Collections now communicates genuine value:

- actual gardens discovered out of 12;
- real cleared/total levels for each collection;
- the actual first level that unlocks a locked collection;
- a direct `Play Level` action;
- earned achievement count.

No fake completion percentages or new production economy were added. Treasure Trail remains milestone-based and idempotent. Real payments, ads and Supabase remain disabled.

The screen hierarchy and tactile response are IMPLEMENTED. Final professional-quality judgment remains PLAYER VISUAL APPROVAL PENDING.
