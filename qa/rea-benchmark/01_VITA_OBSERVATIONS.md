# Vita Mahjong observations

Source: public Google Play listing for `com.vitastudio.mahjong`, inspected 2026-10-23.

## Verified listing-level observations

- The listing presents large, readable Mahjong tiles and a deliberately accessible interface.
- Its described play is exposed-pair matching rather than tray match-three.
- Hint, undo, shuffle and offline play are advertised.

## Not observed

No installed runtime was accessible. Exact tile dimensions, touch latency, animation curves, draw-call behavior, texture sizes, audio mix, save format and device performance were not measured. Any implementation decisions in Jade Garden Tiles are therefore independent design decisions informed only by broad public-facing usability direction.

Confidence: high for the public listing claims; unavailable for runtime internals.
