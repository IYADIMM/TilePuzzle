# Remaining publication blockers

## Owner approval required

1. Create/control the upload key and enroll in Play App Signing.
2. Supply legal/operator name, public support email, privacy-policy hosting URL and any required address.
3. Decide the Play target audience and complete the content-rating questionnaire.
4. Approve native/store artwork and the final listing.
5. Approve the local-only/no-backup save policy.

## Physical/device evidence pending

1. Signed install, launch, long-play and update tests on real Android hardware.
2. Affordable Samsung Galaxy A-series performance, thermal, memory and touch verification.
3. Android 7/API 24 floor and Android 16/API 36 edge-to-edge/predictive-back verification.
4. Actual Android screenshots and Play pre-launch report.

## Blocked until the above

- A final signed AAB cannot be produced honestly without owner-controlled credentials.
- Publication cannot be declared ready without legal/store declarations and device evidence.
- Browser automation proves game behavior and layout, not Android WebView, OEM, thermal or store behavior.

## Deferred by scope

- Cloud save, accounts, Supabase, analytics, crash reporting, ads, billing and real online leaderboards.
- Platform backup until WebView database restore is explicitly engineered and validated.
- Non-blocking cleanup: Vite main-chunk splitting, Capacitor/Gradle deprecation warnings, development-only npm audit advisories, and Android lint warnings for portrait lock/legacy generated resources/launcher-shape heuristics. Android lint has zero errors.
