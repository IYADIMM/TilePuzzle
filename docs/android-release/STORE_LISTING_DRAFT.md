## Title

Ivory Mahjong

## Short description

Relax with layered match-two puzzles and two beautifully crafted tile styles.

## Full description

Settle into a polished offline tile puzzle built around a deceptively simple challenge: uncover free tiles, match pairs, and keep the four-slot tray under control.

Choose Illustrated Garden artwork or an original Classic Mahjong style, then play the same carefully seeded journey in the look you prefer. Twelve illustrated collections, expressive materials, local achievements and records give every chapter its own character.

Features:

- Layered match-two play with clear top and side blocking rules
- Exactly four tray slots for focused, strategic decisions
- Two complete tile styles, switchable without losing progress
- 1,000 deterministic level configurations with progressive challenge
- Hint, shuffle and undo boosters
- Local achievements, rewards and personal records
- Optional music, sound effects and haptics
- Offline play with no account, ads or real-money purchases
